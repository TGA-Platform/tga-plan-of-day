1 Logging In & Selecting Your Centre

Open your browser and go to plan.tga.edu.au.

The login screen at plan.tga.edu.au
  1. Enter your TGA email address (e.g. wollongong@tga.edu.au).
  2. Enter your password and click Sign in.
  3. You will land on the Ratio Dashboard for your centre.
⚠️ Forgotten password? Contact your Area Manager or TGA support. Do not share login credentials with staff.

Selecting Date

The date defaults to today. Use the date picker at the top of the page to view any past or future date. Data loads automatically — roster data comes from Deputy, attendance from Owna.

💡 Tip: You will see a line like "Owna data as of 9:15 AM" showing when attendance last refreshed. Hit 🔄 Refresh any time to pull the latest data.

2 The Dashboard — Daily Overview

The Plan of the Day tab gives you a live snapshot of every room's ratio status. This is your main working view throughout the day.

Dashboard — room cards, float pool, lunch break plan, and day timeline

Summary Banner (dark green bar at top)

  • Currently present — children signed in right now
  • Booked — total booked for today
  • Currently on shift — staff on the floor right now
  • Rooms at risk — rooms below ratio right now
  • Overall status — Compliant or Action needed

Room Cards

Each room shows its current child count, staff required vs. available, individual staff chips, and a green/red/blue compliance badge. Staff chips can be dragged between rooms — see Section 4.

View Toggles

  • All Day — full day forecast based on booked attendance
  • Currently Present — live view of who is actually signed in
  • Snapshot — right now — locks to the current minute for a fixed check

3 The Ratio Check Grid

Click the Ratio Check tab to see a detailed time-slot grid — every room across the top, every time slot down the left. This is where you plan your day and check compliance at each slot.

Ratio Check grid — Morning session. Red spare values mean staff shortage; green means surplus.

Reading the Grid

  • Children count — auto-populated from Owna. Tap any number to override if needed.
  • Required — staff needed based on the age breakdown and NSW ratios
  • Staff chips — who is assigned to that room at that time
  • Spare column — positive = surplus (blue); negative = short (red)

4 Moving Staff Between Rooms

You can reassign any staff member to a different room by tapping their chip. This is a planning overlay only — nothing changes in Deputy.

After tapping a chip, a yellow banner appears — tap any room to place the staff member there, or Cancel.
  1. Tap the staff member's chip (e.g. "Jasmine R.").
  2. A yellow banner appears: "Moving [Name] — tap a room or zone to place".
  3. Tap the destination room. The chip moves and ratio recalculates instantly.
  4. Changed your mind? Tap Cancel in the yellow banner.

Undoing a Move

Tap the staff member's chip in their new location and click the ✕ button next to their name to remove the override and return them to their Deputy-rostered room.

⚠️ Planning only: Moving staff in the Plan of Day does not affect Deputy, payroll, or rostering — it is a visual planning tool for your day.

5 Float & ISS Staff — Plan Day Schedules

Floats and ISS staff have a dedicated scheduling tool — the Plan Day drawer — so you can plan exactly where they'll be across the day, who they're covering for lunch, and when they take their own break.

The Plan Day drawer for a float — showing ratio cover blocks and lunch cover blocks

Opening the Plan Day

  1. On the Plan of the Day tab, scroll down to the Float Pool section.
  2. Click 📋 Plan day next to the float's name.
  3. A drawer slides in from the right showing their shift and existing plan blocks.

Block Types

✅ Auto-suggestions: The system suggests where to place a float based on which rooms have the biggest ratio gaps. Accept or override as needed.

When you assign a float to cover a lunch in the Plan Day, it automatically appears in the Lunch Break Panel — no double entry needed.

6 Lunch Break Planner

Scroll down on the Plan of the Day tab to find the Lunch Break Plan. It auto-generates a staggered lunch schedule for all room staff based on your centre's break rules and available floats.

Lunch Break Plan — staff, scheduled break times, and who is covering them

Overriding a Lunch Time

  1. Click the time shown next to a staff member's name.
  2. Type the new start time (e.g. 11:30). End time adjusts automatically.
  3. Click Save to persist to the Reg 151 compliance record.

Lunch Alerts

If a staff member's planned lunch time passes without a break being recorded, a lunch alert banner appears at the top of the screen with their name and how many minutes overdue.

⚠️ Regulation 151: Staff must take their break within their shift. The system warns you if a break is outside the allowed window. The lunch schedule supports your Reg 151 compliance records.

7 Staff Time Overrides & Overtime

When actual start/finish times differ from the Deputy roster — early arrival, running late, actual lunch break recorded — you can override them directly in the Plan of Day.

Time editor — adjust start/finish, record actual lunch break, flag overtime, add a note
  1. In the Ratio Check grid, click the ⏱ icon next to a staff member's chip.
  2. Adjust Start and/or Finish times.
  3. Optionally record their actual Break start and Break end.
  4. Tick Overtime if they are staying beyond their rostered end time.
  5. Add a Note if useful (e.g. "Covering absent staff").
  6. Click Save.
ℹ️ Deputy sync: Break times auto-populate from Deputy when staff clock their break via the Deputy kiosk. Manual overrides always take priority and will not be overwritten by Deputy data refreshes.

8 Family Groupings

Family Groupings combine two or more rooms for a specific time period. Children and staff from all grouped rooms are pooled together for ratio calculation.

Common Uses

  • Early morning / late afternoon when rooms combine due to low numbers
  • Formal Family Grouping programs (Regulation 168B)
  • Any planned session where multiple age groups share a space

Adding a Family Grouping

  1. On the Ratio Check tab, click 🏠 Family Groupings.
  2. Click + Add Family Grouping.
  3. Give it a label (e.g. "Early morning FG").
  4. Select the rooms to include (leave all unchecked to span the whole centre).
  5. Select the time slots when the grouping applies.
  6. Optionally set Held in room — where children are physically gathered.
  7. Click Save. A coloured band appears across the selected slots in the grid.
🚨 Legal document: Family groupings are recorded under Regulation 168B and saved to the TGA database. Do not delete them retrospectively unless there was a genuine data entry error — contact your Area Manager if unsure.

Each grouping is shown in a distinct colour (purple, blue, green, brown, or red) so multiple groupings on the same day are easy to tell apart.

9 Visitor Log, Comments & Other Features

Visitor Log

Record anyone entering a room who is not a rostered staff member — observers, placement students, contractors, area managers.

  1. Hover over a room cell in the Ratio Check grid and click the 👁 visitor icon.
  2. Enter the visitor's name and entry time, then click Add Visitor.
  3. When they leave, open the visitor log again and click Record Exit.
⚠️ Note: Visitors do not count toward ratio. WWCC compliance is managed separately — the visitor log is an operational record only.

Slot Comments

Add a timestamped note to any time slot — useful for documenting ratio incidents, late pickups, or anything that should be on the record.

  1. Click the 💬 comment icon at the left of any time slot row.
  2. Type your note and click outside or press Enter.

Day Timeline Chart

At the bottom of the Plan of the Day tab, the Day Timeline chart shows total children vs. staff required vs. rostered across the whole day in 15-minute intervals. The red shaded area shows any ratio gap — use it to spot pinch points before they happen.

External Casuals (Z Staffing)

Staff booked through Z Staffing appear with an orange EC badge. Their estimated shift cost is shown on their chip. ISS and support staff appear in the float pool alongside regular floats.

11 NSW Ratio Rules Reference

Age GroupRatioExample
0 – 24 months (under 2s)1 : 48 babies = 2 staff minimum
24 – 36 months (2–3s)1 : 510 toddlers = 2 staff minimum
36 months – 6 years (3–6s)1 : 1020 kindy = 2 staff minimum

Cascade Rule (Mixed Age Rooms)

The younger age group's ratio applies first. Any spare capacity carries over to the older group.

Example: A room with 4 under-2s and 8 two-to-threes.
Step 1: 4 ÷ 4 = 1 staff covers all 4 under-2s (no leftover capacity).
Step 2: 8 ÷ 5 = 2 staff (rounded up).
Total: 3 staff required.

What Counts as "On Ratio"

  • Staff must be physically present in the room
  • Float staff count only when their Plan Day block has them in that room
  • Staff on meal break do not count
  • Staff doing off-floor programming do not count
  • Visitors, students on placement, and ISS/inclusion support do not count
🚨 If a room shows Short: Act immediately — move a surplus staff member in, recall someone from break (give it to them later), or move children to another room. Record the incident in the slot comment.

12 Troubleshooting & FAQ

A staff member isn't on the roster

Check their shift is in Deputy today and in the correct unit for your centre. If it looks correct, click 🔄 Refresh — roster data caches for up to 5 minutes. Still missing? Contact TGA support.

Children counts are wrong

Attendance comes from Owna. Sign the child in via Owna, then hit Refresh. You can also manually override any cell count by clicking the number.

Family groupings disappeared

Check the correct centre and date are selected. If they are genuinely missing, contact TGA support — they can be recovered from the database.

Page won't load or shows an error

  • Check your internet connection
  • Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
  • Try Chrome or Edge if using another browser
  • Contact TGA support with a screenshot of the error

My changes aren't saving

If you see "Save failed", your connection may have dropped. The system retries automatically when connection returns. Don't close the tab until you see "Saved" confirmation.

Can I use this on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Tablet in landscape mode works best. On a phone you may need to scroll horizontally to see all room columns in the Ratio Check grid.
